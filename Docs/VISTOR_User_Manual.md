# VISTOR User Manual  
  
**Version:** 0.8.1  
  
---  
  
## In Plain Terms (Start Here)  
  
VISTOR turns a screen into an old-fashioned cable TV box — the kind you'd have  
used in the 1990s or early 2000s.  
  
Here's the big idea: **you don't pick a specific show to watch.** Just like real  
old TV, you turn it on and something is already playing. You change the channel  
to see what else is "on" right now. If you tune away and come back, the show has  
kept going without you — nothing pauses and waits.  
  
### Turning it on  
  
Plug it in and switch it on. After it starts up, a channel begins playing on its  
own. There is nothing to log into and no menu to pick a movie from.  
  
### Changing channels  
  
- **Channel up / down** moves you to the next or previous channel.  
- **Number buttons** let you type a channel number. Press the number, then the  
  "Enter"/"OK" button to jump straight there.  
- **Previous** (or "Last") jumps back to the channel you were just watching.  
  
Every time you change the channel, a small banner appears in the corner showing  
the channel number and name, then fades away on its own.  
  
### Volume and mute  
  
- **Volume up / down** makes it louder or quieter. A little volume bar appears  
  while you adjust it.  
- **Mute** silences the sound; press it again to bring the sound back.  
  
### The on-screen guide  
  
Press the **Guide** button to see a list of all the channels and what's playing  
on each one. Use up/down to move through the list, and close it to go back to  
watching.  
  
### Settings  
  
Press the **Settings** button to open a small on-screen menu you can change with  
the arrow buttons. The most important setting is the **broadcast mode**, which  
controls how commercials show up:  
  
- **Off** — no commercials at all. Programs play one after another.  
- **Between Programs** — commercials play only in the gap after one show ends  
  and before the next begins.  
- **Mid-Program** — commercials interrupt a show partway through, the way real  
  TV did, then the show continues.  
  
Close the settings menu to save your choice.  
  
### The clock  
  
Press the **Clock** button to briefly show the current time on screen. It fades  
away after a few seconds.

## Everyday Questions  
  
**Can I rewind or pause?**  
No. VISTOR is meant to feel like live TV — whatever is playing is playing now.  
  
**Why did the channel keep going while I was away?**  
That's on purpose. Every channel runs on its own schedule in real time, just  
like broadcast television.  
  
**Nothing is playing on a channel.**  
Some channels may not have programming loaded yet. Try another channel.

| Channel Up/Down | Next / previous channel                       |  
| Number + Enter  | Jump directly to a channel number             |

| Mute            | Silence / un-silence                          |  
| Guide           | Open the on-screen channel guide              |  
| Settings        | Open the on-screen settings menu              |  
| Clock           | Briefly show the current time                 |
